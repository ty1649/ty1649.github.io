3-Wheeled Electric Go-Kart

3-Wheeled Electric Go-Kart is a pirate-themed, single-wheel-steered electric go-kart built with my team for an engineering class, working from wheels, 80/20 extrusions, round stock, an electric motor, and a limited budget.

Outcome

  • Delivered a fully operational 3-wheeled go-kart built from 130+ custom-designed components, with a novel single-wheel steering mechanism that achieved significant weight savings over competing teams' designs while meeting all project requirements.
  • The pirate theme carried through to custom-designed pedals, a pirate ship wheel for steering, and trident-like drivetrain components.

What I Did

My role involved dividing the work among team members for different subassemblies -- chassis, steering, drivetrain, cockpit (gas/brake pedals), and integration -- and I collaborated on the top assembly and contributed to the CAD design, which comprised over 130 components.

  • Designed a bevel-gear-based system for single-wheel steering in the front, using 3D printed parts and fasteners, along with a custom plasma-cut gusset for alignment (we couldn't afford a U-Joint).
  • Worked on the chassis, featuring FEA-analyzed 80/20 gussets, secured with plasma-cut gussets and fasteners.
  • Designed the FDM 3D printed drivetrain components connecting the battery-powered BLDC motors to the rear wheels.
  • Designed the brakes to engage with a bike brake system.
  • Kept the chassis as compact as possible for weight savings, based on the measurements of our shortest team member.
