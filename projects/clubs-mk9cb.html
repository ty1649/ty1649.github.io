CV Boots and Mold

CV Boots and Mold is a set of custom-geometry CV boots and a multi-piece 3D-printed mold I designed for Bruin Formula Racing after no off-the-shelf boot would clear the car's suspension joints without rubbing on the tripod housings and half shafts.

Outcome

  • Scored a bonus point at the FSAE competition for the design.
  • Sealed the CV joint, making the car rules-legal and preventing mechanical failure at the joint.

What I Did

After testing four candidate materials, I chose PMC 770 for its 30-minute pouring time and 70A durometer. The boots were ribbed and hollow, which meant the inner mold couldn't be pulled without destroying it -- I solved this by splitting the inner mold into sections that slide out and collapse for removal, landing on a six-piece mold after many iterations. Pouring had its own problem: a 10-minute working time. I found a working process of 5-6 minutes of vacuum-chamber degassing combined with tilting the mold during the pour to minimize bubbles.
