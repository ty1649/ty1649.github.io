Self Balancing Robot

Self Balancing Robot is a cute, compact two-wheeled robot I designed and built to balance itself using PID control, aiming to make it as small as possible.

What I Did

I applied circuit theory in real-life scenarios along with hand calculations to spec out motors, power source, and microcontrollers necessary -- then I used circuit theory and datasheets to connect them all together and prototype on a breadboard. I also focused on the mounting and placement of electrical systems to fit compactly within a form factor, while also being easily printed and assembled. Finally, I used PID control to balance the robot.

Through this project, I gained hands-on skills using the Arduino Nano alongside electrical components, crimping/soldering/assembling electrical components, surface modeling, and PID control.
