Fingerprint Door Unlocker

Fingerprint Door Unlocker is an Arduino-driven mechanism I designed to unlock my dorm door, after getting tired of paying $20 each time I lost a key card, moving the handle 40 degrees (about 4 inches of linear travel) with roughly 1.5 foot-pounds of force.

What I Did

I started by measuring and planning the specifications for the project. I decided to use an old DC motor found in the junk, which spins at 550 RPM; to increase the torque, I implemented a lead screw linear actuation system. I designed and 3D printed components, fastened with m5/m3 screws, and used a bearing and an L298N motor driver to power the motor. The assembly was coded on Arduino and mounted onto the door with command strips. To account for the circular motion of the door handle, I created an end effector with a claw and a print-in-place hinge.

The finished device opened the door in less than 3 seconds.
