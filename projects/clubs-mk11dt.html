Mk 11 Drivetrain Lead

Mk 11 is Bruin Formula Racing's Formula SAE car; as Drivetrain Lead, I owned the concept-through-competition development of its drivetrain system.

Outcome

  • Helped the car achieve its highest-ever design score and highest-ever event score in team history.
  • First-ever completion of autocross, skidpad, and endurance runs.

Design Review

What I Did

  • Led a 20-member team building the drivetrain, from concept through competition.
  • Developed new torque-transfer and chain-tensioning systems, using topology optimization and nonlinear FEA to cut drivetrain mass by 25% and rotational inertia by 37%.
  • Validated the design through component and vehicle testing, using the results to improve wear resistance, fit, and durability at critical interfaces.
