Hydrogen Fuel Cell

Hydrogen Fuel Cell is UCLA Supermileage Vehicle's self-designed PEM open-cathode fuel cell; as Hydrogen Lead, I directed its design, analysis, and manufacture and integrated it to supply continuous power to the car's wheels.

Outcome

The research behind the fuel cell's parameters and design approach, documented in a slideshow (past designs and timelines were also tracked in a Notion workspace):

What I Did

  • Divided the subteam into two specialized groups: MEA, focusing on chemistry and materials science, and Hardware, focusing on the mechanical components and analysis.
  • Coordinated with the MEA team, which collaborated with the Huang Lab to develop cost-effective membrane coating techniques.
  • Directed the Hardware team's design and analysis of fuel cell components, considering factors like mass transfer, cooling, and clamping force.
  • Provided guidance on technical challenges, set the subteam's trajectory, and managed communication with sponsors and other teams.

Running the subteam through this project built my leadership skills and gave me insight into team management, technical analysis, and cross-disciplinary collaboration.
